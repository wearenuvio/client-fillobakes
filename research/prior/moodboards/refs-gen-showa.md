## Notes for the direction

- Kiln-red holds as a single ink across vehicle, package, print and signage. Nothing here needed a second colour except the tenugui.
- The bilingual lock-up (Latin brush logotype + katakana フィロ・ベイクス + 焼きたてパン tagline) recurred unprompted in every frame — it is effectively the system's signature.
- The loaf-in-a-roundel crest survived at every scale, from van door to matchbox card.
- gs-3 and gs-6 are the asset-level references; gs-1 and gs-5 are the environment proofs; gs-4 is the channel proof.
