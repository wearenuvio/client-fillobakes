# Moodboard Direction — MASCOT-LED / KAWAII / CHARACTER F&B

**Client:** Fillo Bakes — Japanese eggless moving-bakery van, Bengaluru
**Direction shorthand:** "Lucky Chan" — a character *is* the brand. Bold flat graphics, a face people greet, and a van that reads as the mascot's body rather than a truck with a logo stuck on it.

## Suggested palette

Flat, high-chroma, few colours — it has to survive vinyl wrap, direct sun, and a 3-second glance from a moving auto.

| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary — Beckoning Red | `#E8402F` | Hero colour; the van body. Reads at distance, references maneki-neko collar & Japanese sign red. |
| Secondary — Custard Yellow | `#FFC93C` | Egglessness played as warmth, not absence. Type and highlights. |
| Accent — Sakura Pink | `#FF7BA4` | The kawaii lever; mascot cheeks, sprinkles, social frames. |
| Accent — Matcha | `#7FB069` | The one "Japanese ingredient" cue; keeps it from reading purely as a burger brand. |
| Ground — Rice Cream | `#FFF4E2` | Packaging base, negative space, box interiors. |
| Ink — Sumi Navy | `#182C46` | Outlines and body copy. Warmer and friendlier than pure black. |

Optional metallic for luck cues: **Koban Gold `#D9A441`** — coin motif, use sparingly.

## Three keywords

1. **BECKONING** — the mascot's core gesture and the van's core behaviour: it comes to you and waves you over.
2. **PILLOW-SOFT** — rounded, weightless, eggless-light forms; no hard corners anywhere in the system.
3. **ROVING STOREFRONT** — the character has a body, and the body has wheels; every stop is the mascot showing up.
