# Brand Kit Refs — CATCH THE VAN (route-as-game)

Fillo Bakes brand-kit asset cards for the "Catch the Van" direction. Generated via ChatGPT image generation, single chat session, sequential prompts (each after the first begins "same brand system" to hold visual continuity).

**Palette:** mint `#7ED9A7`, sky `#7EC8E3`, capsule yellow `#FFD23F`, vermilion `#D33A2C`, cream `#FFF7E6`
**Motifs:** playful map, dotted routes, pins, stamp rally, collectible bread characters (Croissy, Melon Pan, Happy Toast, Cinnamon Swirl, Choco Chunk, Rolly)
**Files:** `img/kit-game/` — all 1536 x 1024 PNG (3:2)

---

## kg-1.png — Logo presentation card

**Prompt:**
> brand logo presentation card: bold rounded wordmark 'Fillo Bakes' in vermilion with a small location-pin dot over the i, beneath it a circular badge of a smiling bakery van avatar with headlight eyes, playful flat game style, mint and cream card, 3:2

**What rendered:** Cream card on a mint border. Bold rounded vermilion "Fillo Bakes" wordmark set on one line with a vermilion location pin standing in for the dot over the i, flanked by small speed/motion ticks. Below sits a circular mint badge ringed in vermilion holding a front-facing bakery van with big headlight eyes and a smile, bread loaf on the roof and a striped awning. Two supporting text lozenges either side: "FRESH BAKES ON WHEELS" / "BAKED WITH LOVE, DAILY", plus a footer strip "FRESH · LOCAL · MADE WITH CARE". Picked from an A/B pair — the alternate stacked the wordmark over two lines with a smaller, less distinct van badge.

Seven round tokens in a 4-over-3 grid, each in a mint ring with a name plate beneath: Croissy (croissant), Melon Pan, Happy Toast, Cinnamon Swirl on the top row; Choco Chunk (rendered as a chocolate-chip cookie rather than a chunk of bread), Rolly, and the Fillo Van on the bottom. The van token is treated as the rare — wider capsule-yellow frame, sparkles, a vermilion "RARE" plate and a side flag reading "RARE! ONLY 1 IN 7 TOKENS". Footer strip "FRESH BAKES · HAPPY FACES". Single generation, no A/B.

## kg-4.png — Pattern and stamp card

**Prompt:**
> same brand system: pattern and stamp card — left half a seamless map pattern tile: dotted routes, tiny pins, bread icons and a little van on mint; right half a row of four round rally stamps (neighbourhood names INDIRANAGAR, KORAMANGALA, HSR, WHITEFIELD) in vermilion ink style, 3:2

**What rendered:** Split card. Left half is a dense mint pattern tile: white dashed routes winding through scattered vermilion map pins, bread icons (toast, croissant, melon pan, cinnamon roll, cookie, boule) and two small delivery vans — reads as a repeatable pattern. Right half is cream with the "Fillo Bakes" wordmark and a mint ribbon "NEIGHBOURHOOD RALLY STAMPS / FOLLOW · FIND · COLLECT", holding four round vermilion ink-style stamps with neighbourhood skyline illustrations inside: Indiranagar, Koramangala, HSR, Whitefield. Footer strip "ONE CITY. MANY FRESH BAKES."

**Deviation:** the four stamps landed as a 2x2 grid rather than the single row the prompt asked for. Picked from an A/B pair — the alternate did give a true row of four but its left half was an illustrated map scene rather than a seamless mint pattern tile, and the stamp names were much smaller. Traded stamp layout for pattern fidelity and legibility.
