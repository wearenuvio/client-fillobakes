## B. TRACKER / MAP UI — making "where is the van" feel good

### 3. foodpanda — order tracking flow redesign (case study)
Best-in-class study of the *waiting* problem: illustrated rider states, an ETA range instead of
a false-precision minute, an inline "contact your rider" chat, and honest status copy
("We've got you a rider! They're heading to the restaurant"). Fillo's van tracker is the same
emotional problem — reduce uncertainty while someone waits.

### 4. FirstClub — animated order-tracking illustrations (Lottie)
Character-led status animations that turn "assigning a rider" dead-time into a little scene.
The stated goal is literally Fillo's: "transform routine status updates into a delightful
visual experience… reinforce progress, reduce uncertainty." Model for a Fillo van mascot who
does something different at each tracker state (loading / driving / parked / sold out).

## C. DROP / COUNTDOWN COMMERCE — Hotplate is the closest thing to Fillo's business model

### 5. Hotplate — drop list with live countdown starburst ★ STRONGEST
The single most transferable screen in this whole board. A bakery's upcoming pickups as stacked
cards: hero photo, "Sunday Pickup", pin + venue name, a greyed "Dropping soon!" button, and a
torn-edge starburst sticker reading "DROP IN 00:29" sitting half-off the card. Below it, a live
"17 people 🔥" scarcity chip and a red "Order now!" once open. Swap "Pickup" for "Van stop" and
this IS the Fillo homepage.
IMG: https://framerusercontent.com/images/ChKhkWcGirFFRjE1d7beigIeb8.png (1456×1396)
Source: https://hotplate.com/

### 6. Hotplate — SMS/notification commerce layer
Chat bubbles floating over a food photo: "Butter and Crumble is about to drop!", "Your order
from Butter & Crumble is ready to be picked up!", "Sweet! You're officially on the drop list."
This is exactly the WhatsApp tone Fillo needs — transactional messages written as if a person
sent them, plus the visual device of bubbles as a hero graphic.

"Inventory 50", "Max per order", "Max per pickup time". The scarcity math made visible and

## E. MAP / TRACKER UI WITH CHARM

### 11. Transit — live route screen ★ STRONGEST for the van tracker
The reference for "GPS with a face." A sunglasses-emoji avatar rides the route line on the map;
a bus glyph shows the vehicle's actual position; each arrival card carries a broadcast-wave mark
that means *this number is live, not a timetable*; the minute count is set enormous; and the
primary action is a fat blue "GO". Everything Fillo needs for a van that is genuinely moving.
IMG: https://framerusercontent.com/images/zbbyDGckpSvplAv6XykC5NHE.jpg (750×1480)
Source: https://transitapp.com/

### 12. Transit — "Leave now" trip screen with live pill
Same system in orange: an "ARRIVE AT 9:54" floating pill with a live-wave glyph, a "32s"
countdown bubble pinned to the vehicle, a dotted walk segment, and a horizontally scrollable rail
of next departures (3 / 6 / 8 minutes). The countdown-attached-to-a-moving-dot pattern is exactly
"the Fillo van is 4 min from your street."
IMG: https://framerusercontent.com/images/EesCgityYjsYGNUMdz2yy97Xyuk.jpg (750×1480)
Source: https://transitapp.com/

## PATTERNS WORTH STEALING

1. **The countdown sticker that overlaps the card.** Hotplate's torn-edge "DROP IN 00:29"
   starburst sits half off the pickup card, breaking the grid on purpose — and it is paired with
   a live scarcity chip ("17 people 🔥") and a deliberately *disabled* "Dropping soon!" button.
   Three states of one button (locked → live → sold out) plus one sticker does all the work of a
   drop mechanic. Fillo's van stops are drops; build the card once and restate it everywhere.

2. **"Live" as a glyph, not a word.** Transit puts a tiny broadcast-wave mark next to every
   minute count so a real-time GPS number is visually distinguishable from a scheduled one, and
   sticks a "32s" bubble directly onto the moving vehicle dot. Fillo's tracker should mark the
   difference between *the van is really here* and *the van is due here* with a mark, plus give
   the van a face on the map the way Transit gives the rider an emoji avatar.

3. **One hue per flavour, carried across every surface.** Ginza ni Shikawa floods the entire hero
   — wall, table, ceramics, props — in the colour of that month's loaf. Combined with the
   nine-posts-as-one-artboard grid from the Twinkle Treats system, that gives Fillo a repeatable
   monthly ritual: pick the flavour, pick its hue, and the website hero, the three feed tiles and
   the drop sticker are all generated from the same swatch.
