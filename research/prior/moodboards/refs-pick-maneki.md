## Notes on the direction
- The strongest references run *one* character through every surface rather than a cast — Bunzo's, Noraneko, FOCA. That is the discipline MANEKI POP should hold.
- Two workable line languages emerge: single-weight cream/red line art on a flat ground (pm-08, 09, 23, 25) and chunky filled outline blobs (pm-07, 10, 12). The first reads more Japanese and prints cheaply on a van.
- Mascot-as-costume (pm-04) is a low-cost live activation route for a moving van — worth flagging as a tactic, not just a graphic.
