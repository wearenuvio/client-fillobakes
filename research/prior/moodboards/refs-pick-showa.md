## Three best

1. **ps-05** — shokupan wrapper with the red horse-drawn bread-cart line art. A Showa bakery already drawing its own delivery vehicle on the pack; this is the Fillo van as a one-colour wrapper mark, prior art and all.
2. **ps-23** — いわみパン祭り bread-festival poster. Single hero loaf, pale grid, date/venue micro-type. The exact template for a Fillo "where the van is this week" poster or story frame.
3. **ps-16** — indigo tenugui with three crest roundels. Shows how one brand mark scales into a family of circular crests on cloth: merch, van door decal, and stamp-card mark from a single system.

## Notes for assembly

- **Two-colour discipline** is the through-line: ps-05, ps-09, ps-10, ps-15, ps-17 all hold with a single ink plus paper. Cheap to print in Bengaluru, unmistakable on a van.
- **The roundel** recurs across cells (ps-01 brand mark, ps-16 crests, ps-21 badge) — strongest candidate for the Fillo primary mark.
- **ps-14 + ps-18** give a ready pattern library with proper names; pick 2-3 (asanoha, seigaiha, kagome) as the secondary system for boxes, liners, and the van's lower panel.
- **ps-30** is the useful bridge: brochure print and vehicle drawn in the same line weight — a good model for the website's van illustration.
