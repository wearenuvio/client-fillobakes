# Gen refs — CATCH THE VAN (route-as-game)

Direction: route-as-game. Palette: mint #7ED9A7, sky #7EC8E3, capsule yellow #FFD23F, vermilion #D33A2C, cream.
Client: Fillo Bakes — moving bakery van with live GPS tracker, Bengaluru.
Source: ChatGPT image generation, 2026-08-26. Files in `img/gen-game/`.

---

## gg-1.png — Van wrap mockup
**Prompt:** playful bakery van wrap mockup: mint and sky-blue city-map pattern with dotted route lines and location pins, big vermilion rounded logo 'Fillo Bakes', large QR plate on the side, bright flat design, van parked on a colourful street, 3:2

**Rendered:** Photoreal mint/sky van on a colourful street — full-side city-map wrap with white street grid, red dotted route and drop pins, vermilion rounded "Fillo Bakes" wordmark with a circular bread roundel, red-and-white striped awning, and a large white QR plate ("SCAN TO ORDER") on the rear panel plus small bread-character spot icons. 1536x1024.

## gg-2.png — Collectible-pack bakery box
**Prompt:** bakery box mockup designed like a collectible game pack: numbered edition badge, 'sticker inside' starburst, route-map pattern printed inside the lid, mint sky-blue and capsule-yellow palette, bold rounded 'Fillo Bakes' type, studio photo, 3:2

**Rendered:** Studio shot on marble of an open mint box with a printed route map inside the lid (pins + red dashed route), vermilion logo, a yellow starburst "STICKER INSIDE!", a yellow "No. 0417 / OF 1000 / COLLECTOR'S EDITION" badge on the front panel, four bakes inside. Strongest packaging artefact of the set. 1536x1024.

## gg-3.png — Stamp-rally kit flat lay
**Prompt:** flat lay of a bakery stamp-rally kit: passport-style booklet with neighbourhood pages, round rubber stamps, collectible bread-character stickers, punch card, mint and vermilion palette, playful Japanese stationery style, 3:2

**Rendered:** Full kit on cream: mint "STAMP RALLY PASSPORT" cover, open spread with real Bengaluru neighbourhood pages (Indiranagar, Jayanagar) and ghosted stamp slots incl. a "SECRET BAKE!" slot, two wooden rubber stamps, a bread-character sticker sheet, a 6-step punch card ("COLLECT 6 STAMPS"), a canvas pouch and a coaster. Carries incidental Japanese subtitles from the "Japanese stationery" cue — drop or replace with Kannada for real use. 1536x1024.

## gg-4.png — Live tracker app screen
**Prompt:** mobile app screen mockup: live bakery van tracker as a cute game map, van avatar with a face moving along a dotted route through Bengaluru neighbourhoods, ETA bubble, 'notify me' button, soft flat game UI, mint and cream, phone mockup, 2:3

**Rendered:** iPhone mockup on mint: illustrated game-style Bengaluru map with landmark buildings, red dashed route through Malleswaram / Halasuru / Indiranagar / Basavanagudi / Jayanagar / Koramangala, smiling van avatar mid-route, a vermilion "ETA 12 min / 2.4 km away" bubble, a "We're near you!" card with a red NOTIFY ME button, and a bottom tab bar: TRACK / MENU / STAMP RALLY / ORDERS. 1024x1536 (portrait). Best single artefact for the tracker mechanic.
