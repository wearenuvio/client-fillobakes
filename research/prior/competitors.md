**Client**: Fillo Bakes (https://www.fillobakes.com/) — Bengaluru

**Method**: Web search + page fetches (public sources only). ## Tier 1 — Direct competitors (Japanese bread / shokupan, reachable by a Bengaluru buyer)

### 1. Shokupan "Micro Bakery" — *the single most direct competitor*
- **Website**: https://www.shokupaninmicrobakery.com/ (also referenced as shokupan.in)
- **City/reach**: Whitefield, Bengaluru. Delivery/pickup, Bengaluru only.
- **Positioning**: Single-product specialist micro-bakery — Japanese milk bread made with the Yudane/Tangzhong method, baked in small batches daily. Founder: Siddhant Verma, ex-hotelier ("Founder and Bread Artist").
- **Product range vs Fillo**: Narrower and deeper. ~9 shokupan varieties (classic, raisin, beetroot, dark chocolate walnut, plus vegan options). No kare pan / an pan / pies found. Fillo has broader Japanese bakery range (100+ SKUs claimed).
- **Prices**: Not published anywhere public — quoted over WhatsApp/Instagram. *(uncertain)*
- **D2C maturity**: **Low-to-medium.** Has a domain and an "order online" page, but the operative channel is WhatsApp (+91 7349488617) or Instagram DM, with 24-hour advance ordering, Mon–Fri only. No visible Swiggy/Zomato presence.
- **Social**: Instagram @shokupan.in_micro_bakery — ~1,175 followers *(approximate, from search snippet; likely stale)*. LinkedIn presence for the founder.
- **SEO presence**: **This is the problem.** It is the #1 organic result for "shokupan Bangalore" and "Japanese milk bread Bangalore," and it owns the LBB and JustDial editorial coverage for the term. It has effectively claimed the exact keyword Fillo needs.
- **Strengths**: Category-defining local name (the brand *is* the keyword); first-mover SEO and PR; craft credibility; vegan SKUs.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: Mon–Fri only, 24h pre-order, WhatsApp-first ordering, one neighbourhood (Whitefield), no eggless guarantee published, no membership/loyalty, no subscription, no real e-commerce checkout, tiny follower base (Fillo has ~4x the Instagram audience). Product range is single-category.

### 2. Lily Pond Bakery
- **Website**: https://www.lilypondbakery.co.in/
- **City/reach**: Ahmedabad-origin (founder Paresh Patel; European-style artisan bakery). **Delivers in Bengaluru** (delivery-only in Bengaluru; pickup only in Ahmedabad/Vadodara).
- **Positioning**: Ahmedabad's first European-style artisan bakery, now serving Bengaluru — sourdough, viennoiserie, plus a "rich shokupan."
- **Product range vs Fillo**: Broad European artisan range; shokupan is one SKU, not the identity. Some products explicitly offered eggless (e.g. pistachio–sour cherry croissant), but the brand is not eggless-first.
- **Prices**: Product pages render client-side; prices not retrievable. *(unknown)*
- **D2C maturity**: **Medium-high.** Own-site checkout with city selection, published delivery schedule, designated pickup stores.
- **SEO presence**: Ranks on page 1 for "shokupan India"-type queries. Competes with Fillo on shokupan without living in Bengaluru.
- **Strengths**: Real e-commerce infrastructure, multi-city, published delivery calendar, established craft reputation.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: Not a Bengaluru bakery — no same-neighbourhood freshness story, no truck; shokupan is a side SKU; not eggless-positioned.

### 3. Yokohama Bread & Confectionaries — *the biggest latent threat*
- **Website**: https://yokohamabread.com/
- **City/reach**: Mumbai (Powai / Hiranandani). Founded 2019.
- **Positioning**: The most complete Japanese bakery brand in India — explicitly branded on Japanese bread heritage ("a brand with the name from where Japanese bread started in 1860").
- **Product range vs Fillo**: **The closest product-for-product match in the country** — Shoku Pan, An Pan, Curry Pan, Melon Pan, Cream Pan, Coppe Pan. This is Fillo's exact menu architecture, built five years earlier.
- **Prices**: Not published. *(unknown)*
- **D2C maturity**: **Low.** Retail/walk-in led ("Visit Us At"); no visible online checkout, no evidence of Bengaluru delivery.
- **Social/SEO**: Thin. Ranks for "Japanese bread India"-type queries but has not built content depth.
- **Strengths**: Authentic full Japanese pan range, longest tenure in the Indian market, strong brand name for the category.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: No Bengaluru presence, weak digital, no eggless positioning (Japanese pan traditionally uses egg wash), no subscription.
- **Threat**: If they franchise or open Bengaluru, they arrive with a more complete and more credible Japanese story than Fillo. Worth monitoring.

### 4. Tokyo Bakery (Pune) & Yokoso — Japanese Bakehouse (Pune)
- **Websites**: https://www.zomato.com/pune/tokyo-bakery-baner (no strong own site found); https://www.yokosothebakehouse.in/
- **City/reach**: Pune only. Not a Bengaluru competitor today.
- **Positioning**: Tokyo Bakery — Japanese-style bakery, soft shokupan plus Japanese bakes; ~4.5/5 across ~764 reviews *(approximate, Zomato/aggregate)*. Yokoso — "authentic Japanese flavours," soft Japanese buns and cheesecakes.
- **Relevance**: **Benchmark, not rival.** They prove the format works in an Indian metro at scale and give Fillo a preview of the menu/format that wins. Also relevant if Fillo ever pursues pan-India shipping or a second city.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: No Bengaluru delivery, no eggless positioning, no D2C shipping found.

### 5. CrustWorthy Gourmet Breads
- **Website**: https://crustworthybreads.in/products/shokupan-japanese-milk-bread
- **City/reach**: Pune-based, Shopify store with shipping calculated at checkout (coverage area not stated — assume regional/perishable-limited). *(uncertain)*
- **Positioning**: Gourmet artisan bread D2C — sourdough, gluten-free, speciality, sandwich breads, butters.
- **Prices**: **Shokupan ₹152** — a useful public price anchor for the category in India.
- **D2C maturity**: **Medium-high.** Proper Shopify checkout, email capture, category architecture.
- **Strengths**: Real e-commerce stack, breadth (gluten-free line), transparent pricing, ranks for "shokupan India."
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: **Contains egg** — their shokupan lists egg as an ingredient/allergen. This is the sharpest available proof point for Fillo's "eggless shokupan without compromise" claim. Also no Bengaluru freshness, no membership.

## Tier 2 — Eggless-first premium bakeries in Bengaluru (compete on the *eggless* USP)

### 6. IYLO Bakehouse — *closest competitor on the eggless-premium axis*
- **Website**: https://www.iylobakehouse.com/
- **City/reach**: Jayanagar, Bengaluru (single outlet); delivery across Bengaluru, free above ₹999.
- **Positioning**: "Eggless bakery and cake shop in Jayanagar... Served with intent." Contemporary **premium, 100% eggless** bakehouse — the same core claim Fillo makes.
- **Product range vs Fillo**: Celebration cakes, cookies, breads, gourmet desserts, tarts, custom cakes, gifting; viennoiserie "launching soon." **No Japanese/shokupan/milk bread.**
- **Prices**: Not published on the homepage. *(unknown)*
- **D2C maturity**: **Medium-high.** Own-site ordering with delivery/pickup toggle, free-delivery threshold, WhatsApp (+91 7625096799).
- **Social**: Instagram @iylo.bakehouse; Facebook. Follower count not verifiable via search. *(unknown)*
- **SEO presence**: Ranks organically for "eggless artisanal bakery Bangalore" — one of the very few *craft* brands surfacing above the gifting aggregators for that query.
- **Strengths**: Owns "premium eggless" as brand identity, not as a filter; strong design/brand craft; physical destination store; gifting revenue line Fillo lacks.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: Cake/dessert-led, not bread-led; no Japanese angle; one neighbourhood, no doorstep-route model; no membership/loyalty; high ₹999 free-delivery threshold vs Fillo's per-item ₹99–189 impulse pricing.

- **D2C maturity**: **Medium-high.** Own site + AirMenus, same-day delivery, store locator.
- **Social**: Instagram @amintiriindia; Facebook amintiriindia. *(follower count unknown)*
- **Strengths**: Premium price realisation, multi-outlet, strong gifting/hamper business, ranks for "eggless" queries.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: Eggless is a sub-collection, not a promise — Fillo can attack on "100% eggless kitchen, no cross-contact." No Japanese range; no subscription.

### 8. Happy Belly Bakes
- **Website**: https://happybellybakes.com/collections/eggless-collection
- **City/reach**: Bengaluru, 3 outlets, 2-hour delivery citywide.
- **Positioning**: Handcrafted luxury patisserie since 2009; eggless and gluten-free versions of flagship cakes; has diversified into meal boxes, pizzas, burgers.
- **Prices**: Cake-led, premium tier. *(specific prices not captured)*
- **D2C maturity**: **High.** Shopify-class own store + Swiggy + Zomato, instant/2-hour/scheduled delivery.
- **Social**: Instagram @happybellybakes — **~29K followers** *(approximate)*. ~6x Fillo's audience.
- **SEO presence**: Ranks for "eggless cake Bangalore" and has heavy LBB editorial coverage (multiple LBB pages).
- **Strengths**: 15+ years of brand equity, largest social audience in the artisan set, multi-channel delivery, GF + eggless breadth.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: Cake/occasion-led, not daily-bread; menu sprawl (pizzas, meal boxes) dilutes the bakery story; no Japanese products; no membership programme found.

## Tier 3 — Artisan bread D2C in Bengaluru (compete on *fresh bread to your door*)

### 9. Sour House — *closest analogue to Fillo's subscription/route model*
- **Website**: https://sourhouse.in/ (also Zomato: Koramangala 4th Block)
- **City/reach**: Koramangala, Bengaluru — small cafe at the bakery + citywide subscription delivery.
- **Positioning**: Small-batch natural-fermentation sourdough by founder Selvan Thandapani. **Weekly subscription: one loaf per week for a month.**
- **Product range vs Fillo**: Sourdough loaves, crackers, croutons, occasional cinnamon rolls, pizza bases. No Japanese bread, no eggless positioning (sourdough is inherently eggless but not marketed as such).
- **Prices**: LBB indicates **₹180+** entry price point for their bread. *(approximate)*
- **D2C maturity**: **Low-medium.** Subscription runs on enquiry, not self-serve checkout; also on Zomato for ordering.
- **Strengths**: **The only established recurring-delivery bread subscription in Bengaluru** — proves demand for Fillo's Fillo+/route model; strong LBB editorial ("Bread Bingers!"); loyal Koramangala base.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: Manual subscription onboarding, no app/site checkout for the plan, single product category, no Japanese/eggless story, no truck reach.

### 10. Krumbkraft — Sourdough Bakehouse

- **Positioning**: "Freshly baked. No additives. No chemicals." Artisanal sourdough breads, crackers, cakes, preserves.

### 11. Love Made Edible
- **Website**: https://lovemadeedible.in/artisan-breads-bangalore

- **SEO presence**: **Strongest programmatic-SEO play in the local bread set** — a dedicated `/artisan-breads-bangalore` landing page with neighbourhood-level keyword coverage. This is the template Fillo should copy.
- **Strengths**: Broadest neighbourhood SEO footprint; citywide delivery; recurring-order behaviour already established.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: No formalised subscription product; no Japanese/eggless differentiation; no brand-level PR.

### 12. The Anthara
- **Website**: https://www.theanthara.com/breads-and-sourdough

- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: Cake-first brand with bread bolted on; no eggless claim stated; no Japanese range; no loyalty programme.

## Tier 4 — Scale players (compete on *convenience and price*, not craft)

### 13. Theobroma — *the most underrated threat*
- **Website**: https://theobroma.in/collections/breads-in-bangalore
- **City/reach**: National chain, multiple Bengaluru outlets.
- **Positioning**: Mass-premium bakery/patisserie.
- **Prices**: **French Baguette ₹100 (140g), Sourdough ₹120 (350g), Wheat Loaf ₹90 (400g), Multigrain ₹110 (400g)** — and **all four are explicitly marketed as "100% eggless."**
- **D2C maturity**: **Highest in this list.** Own website + own mobile app + Swiggy + Zomato + **Blinkit, Instamart and Zepto**. Quick-commerce means a Bengaluru customer gets eggless artisan-style bread in 10 minutes.
- **Strengths**: Owns "eggless bread, delivered fast, cheap" — the convenience flank of Fillo's positioning; national brand trust; 4–6 day shelf life.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: No shokupan, no Japanese bread, no fresh-baked-after-order story, industrial rather than handmade, no personality/founder story.
- **Why it matters**: Theobroma neutralises "eggless" as a differentiator at the ₹90–120 price band. Fillo's ₹99–189 items must win on *Japanese craft and freshness*, not on eggless alone.

### 14. Lavonne (Café + Academy)
- **Website**: https://lavonnecafe.com/
- **City/reach**: Bengaluru (Indiranagar, Whitefield, Domlur) + New Delhi.
- **Positioning**: French-style patisserie attached to India's best-known baking academy — the prestige benchmark of the Bengaluru bakery scene.
- **Prices**: LBB indicates bread from **₹60+** (sandwich loaf, focaccia, multigrain with seeds). *(approximate)*
- **D2C maturity**: **Medium.** Strong retail and Swiggy/Zomato presence; ordering also via phone/email; less own-site transactional depth than its brand size implies.
- **Social**: Instagram @lavonneacademyindia **~129K** and @lavonnecafe **~42K** *(approximate)*. Dominant share of voice in Bengaluru baking.
- **Strengths**: Enormous authority and audience; eggless celebration products and hampers; institutional credibility.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: French, not Japanese; not eggless-first; no subscription; destination-led rather than doorstep-led.

### 15. Bakingo / CakeZone (cloud-kitchen D2C)
- **Websites**: https://www.bakingo.com/eggless-cakes/bangalore ; CakeZone (Curefoods-owned)
- **Reach**: Bakingo — Gurugram HQ, Series B, **$26.5M raised**, cloud-kitchen model, **all cakes eggless-only**. CakeZone — founded 2016 in Bengaluru, ~29 kitchens across 6 cities, ~$43.9M revenue, acquired by Curefoods Oct 2021. *(figures per Tracxn/Growjo — approximate)*
- **Relevance**: They are **SEO competitors, not product competitors.** They own the "eggless … Bangalore" SERP with programmatic city/flavour landing pages and will outrank any craft bakery on head terms.
- **Weaknesses vs Fillo**: Zero bread, zero craft, zero Japanese. Fillo cannot beat them on "eggless cake Bangalore" and should not try.

## Strategic read

**Where Fillo is genuinely unique (defend these):**
1. **The moving bakery truck.** No Bengaluru competitor found operating a mobile/route bakery. It is the only asset in the set that cannot be replicated by a Shopify theme.
2. **Full Japanese pan range + 100% eggless, together.** Yokohama has the range but not eggless or Bengaluru. Shokupan Micro Bakery has Bengaluru but one product and no eggless claim. CrustWorthy's shokupan literally contains egg. Nobody else occupies the intersection.
3. **Fillo+ membership at a ₹1 entry fee** — the only real self-serve loyalty programme in the local craft set. Sour House proves subscription demand exists; Fillo has better mechanics.
4. **National-tier PR** (The Hindu) that no competitor in Tier 1–3 has matched.

**Where Fillo is losing:**
1. **SEO.** Invisible for "shokupan Bangalore" while a much smaller competitor ranks #1. There is no evidence of neighbourhood landing pages — Love Made Edible and The Anthara both out-execute Fillo here with basic pSEO.
2. **Audience scale.** ~5K IG vs Happy Belly Bakes ~29K and Lavonne ~129K. Fillo does lead its direct Japanese-category rival ~4:1, which is the comparison worth using.
3. **Price/convenience flank.** Theobroma sells 100% eggless bread at ₹90–120 on Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart in 10 minutes. Fillo cannot win on eggless-as-a-feature; it must win on Japanese craft, fresh-baked-to-order, and the route ritual.
4. **Content moat.** "Japanese milk bread India" is owned by nobody — currently Wikipedia and IndiaMART. That is a free category-education lane sitting unclaimed.
