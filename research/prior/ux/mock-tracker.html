The geofence runs on the server, against the van. The alert is anchored to a stop the customer picked, not to their live position, which means no location permission, no background tracking, no battery cost. That is a real difference and it gets said out loud, in plain words, under the button.

Priming, never a cold prompt. The sheet appears after a value moment, twenty seconds of dwell or a second visit, and it shows exactly what the message will say and how often. An explicit "Not now" is part of the pattern; pressured users deny permanently.

Visualised · proof and the game layer

The day generates its own proof. The card is the only opponent.

Nothing in the feed is written by hand. Every line is generated from stop states and the position feed, most recent first, timestamped, capped at five. Sell-out speed, gates rung, a stop returning after months: scarcity and effort, evidenced rather than claimed.

The stamp slot is built now, filled later. Six slots mapped one-to-one onto the six stops, so the game adds no new geography to learn. Stamps are earned at the counter by being there, EKITAG's model, not by tapping a button.

No leaderboards, ever. Foursquare died of extrinsic motivation; per-stop populations here are small enough that competition would be personal. Finite, seasonal, resettable, and the reward is a real bake.
