# Fillo Bakes × Nuvio — Where Your Brand Meets Your Customer
### Touchpoint map & focus plan
_18 August 2026_

---

## How to read this

Every place a customer encounters Fillo — on the road, on a phone, on a doorstep, on a table — is a touchpoint. Each one either confirms the brand or contradicts it. Right now the product, the press, and the checkout are strong; most of the surfaces *around* them are silent or unbranded, so first encounters undersell the bakery.

We mapped the full journey, grounded each touchpoint in published effectiveness data, and sorted everything into three focus buckets. Primary is not "most glamorous" — it is what moves orders fastest **and** compounds the brand.

## The customer journey — two front doors

```
DISCOVER → CONSIDER → ORDER → WAIT → RECEIVE → EAT & SHARE → REPEAT & GIFT
```

- **Digital-first** (today's main path): Instagram / press / Google → website → checkout → doorstep.
- **Street-first** (the path only you have): someone sees the van → QR or handle → follows or buys → becomes a digital regular.

The street-first funnel is almost entirely unbuilt — and it is the one no competitor can copy.

## The focus matrix

### 🔴 PRIMARY — Exist credibly and convert *(weeks 1–4)*
1. **Brand identity system** — everything else builds on it
2. **Instagram relaunch** (van-led, Reels-first)
3. **WhatsApp catalog + Channel**
4. **Google Business Profile + review engine + site SEO basics**
5. **Packaging system** (design phase)
6. *Quick win, week 1:* handwritten note in every first order

**What this bucket solves:** the credibility gap. Today a curious customer checks Instagram (silent), Google (absent), reviews (none) and receives an unbranded bag. After Primary, every first encounter confirms the press instead of contradicting it — and Fillo owns three direct lines to its customers (Instagram, WhatsApp, Google) instead of renting reach from marketplaces at 25–35% commission.

### 🟡 SECONDARY — Weaponize what only Fillo has *(weeks 4–10)*
1. **Van livery** (from the new identity)
2. **Live tracker as a flagship page** + "notify me" list
3. **The weekly drop ritual** (named, capped, waitlisted)
4. **Review/UGC engine + press everywhere**
5. **Product page copy + local search pages**
6. **Creator seeding** (starting with the two warm relationships)
7. **Pincode check + delivery policy rewrite**
8. *Timing item:* Diwali gift SKU locked by mid-October

**What this bucket solves:** differentiation and rhythm. Primary makes Fillo credible; Secondary makes it *unmistakable* — the van, the live pin, the Thursday bake become the reasons people talk about Fillo and come back on schedule. Scarcity baked to confirmed orders also cuts waste, which on a fresh bakery is a direct margin gain.

### 🟢 TERTIARY — Compound and scale *(the quarter)*
1. **Weekly subscription** + society-gate unlock
2. **Fillo+ nurture** + signature perk
3. **Doorstep experience kit** (bell, apron, script)
4. **Referral + gifting programme** (beyond Diwali)
5. **Full site reskin**
6. **YouTube Shorts + LinkedIn** (repurposing engine)
7. **Marketplace listing polish** (Swiggy as discovery, not margin)

**What this bucket solves:** economics. Habit becomes contract — subscriptions lift lifetime value 3–4x, gifting raises order values, referred customers retain 37% better. Marketing cost per order falls while order value rises. This is the moat phase: by the time competitors copy the content, Fillo's customers are on standing orders.

## Why this order (the dependency logic)

```
Identity → van wrap + packaging + feed look + site
Instagram + WhatsApp → the drop → the subscription
Google profile + review loop → every future customer's first check
Tracker → notify-me list → feeds WhatsApp
```

Skipping ahead breaks things: a van wrap before the identity locks a temporary look onto your most permanent asset; a drop before the audience is warm sells out to nobody; a subscription before the drop has no habit to convert.
