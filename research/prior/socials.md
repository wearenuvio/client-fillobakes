## 3. Content-format benchmarks

### What the 2026 data says
- **Healthy IG engagement rate: ~1.5–3%.** Consumer goods/retail Reels average **~2.4%**.
- **Carousels are the top-performing format in Food & Beverage — they beat Reels on engagement.** Recipe/step-by-step carousels outperform single plated-dish photos, because "save this" is a stronger signal than a like.
- Reels + carousels earn **2–3x** the engagement of single static images.
- **Recommended 2026 mix: ~60–70% Reels, ~20–30% carousels**, remainder static/stories.
- Tactical: post at peak hours, **reply to every comment inside the first hour**, niche hashtags over broad ones, build save-worthy content (tips, tutorials, lists).

### Cadence benchmark
| Tier | Cadence | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Category leaders | Near-daily (2,300–2,600 lifetime posts) | Baker's Dozen, SMOOR |
| Strong mid-tier | 1,178 posts | Magnolia India |
| Committed niche | 617–2,531 posts | Shokupan micro-bakeries |
| Playbook rec. for IG | **1–2 posts + daily Stories** | — |
| **Fillo today** | **11 posts lifetime; 0 original in 6 months** | — |

### Short-form video mechanics (from playbook)
- **The 3-second rule** — every video needs `[VISUAL HOOK] + [VERBAL HOOK] + [TEXT OVERLAY]` firing simultaneously in second one.
- **Reels:** 15–30s, 9:16, polished, rewards saves/shares. **Shorts:** 30–60s, YouTube SEO applies — titles are searchable. **TikTok:** 15–60s, raw/authentic, trending sounds.
- **Captions/subtitles lift watch time 25–40%.** Max 2 lines on screen, 3–5 words per line, bold sans-serif with black outline, key words highlighted in a second colour.
- Structures that fit a bakery: **Tutorial** (show the end result first → "here's how" → steps → result + CTA); **Problem-Solution** (hook the problem → agitate → solution → CTA); **List** (one item every 5–8s).
- Top mistakes: slow hooks, no text overlay, bad audio, too long, no CTA, ignoring comments in hour one.
- E-commerce/product formats that map directly: **unboxing, comparisons, how-it's-made, customer reviews.**

### The shokupan/milk-bread trend is live right now
- TikTok has turned Japanese milk bread into a **mainstream UK food craze** — it has jumped from social to supermarket shelves.
- **Waitrose: searches for Japanese milk buns +617% in a month; sales +27%.** Their global range developer called milk buns one of the most interesting social-to-kitchen trend leaps.
- The specific content engine: **jiggle/squish/ASMR** — the pillowy texture is the whole hook. Mukbang and ASMR formats around fluffy Japanese bread are established and performing.
- Native Japanese vocabulary Fillo already owns — **"fuwa fuwa"** — is literally the trend's descriptor and is already in the IG bio but nowhere in the content.

**This is the finding that matters most: Fillo sells the exact product that is trending globally, in a city with almost no direct competition, and is posting nothing.**

## 4. Gaps vs benchmark

### Severity 1 — existential
1. **Six-month content blackout.** Last original post 26 Feb 2026. A trading business with a dead feed reads as closed. Prospective customers checking IG before a first order see abandonment.
2. **Press spike wasted.** The Hindu + Market Spoon + News Karnataka all landed in **March 2026** — *after* the feed went quiet. Peak awareness hit an empty room. The ~5.1K followers are the residue; they have never been given a reason to stay.
3. **Zero review footprint.** No Google rating surfaced, no magicpin rating, no JustDial listing, no Zomato. Swiggy rating unverified. A food brand with no reviews at 8 months has no social proof at the exact moment of purchase decision.

### Severity 2 — structural
4. **Format mix inverted.** 8 static photos : 3 reels, and 2 of those 3 reels are reposts. Benchmark is 60–70% Reels + 20–30% carousels. Fillo has effectively **one** original reel, ever.
5. **The trend is being left on the table.** Shokupan/milk bread is mid-viral-cycle globally (Waitrose +617% search). Jiggle/squish/ASMR is the proven format. Fillo makes it, is eggless (a genuine differentiator and a harder technical feat, i.e. a story), and has shot none of it.
6. **Following = 0.** No outbound engagement whatsoever. The playbook's baseline is 20–50 target accounts engaged consistently. For a neighbourhood-cluster business this is doubly costly — no local creator relationships, no reciprocal reach, no community.
7. **No WhatsApp Channel.** WhatsApp is already the ordering rail. A broadcast Channel is the natural fit for a slot-based moving bakery — "truck is in Indiranagar at 4pm, 12 loaves left" is a perfect push-notification use case, and it's free. Completely unclaimed.
8. **LinkedIn is a showcase page with 5 followers and 2 posts.** Wrong page type (showcase pages under-reach company pages), and the stated B2B/HORECA/catering ambition has no surface to run on. Founder personal profiles are currently outperforming the brand page.
9. **YouTube empty.** No indexed videos. Shorts are searchable — "eggless shokupan recipe", "Japanese milk bread India" are queries with no Indian brand answering them.

### Severity 3 — opportunity
10. **Two warm creator relationships unexploited.** Sharmaji Eats and Sudha Rao already made reels voluntarily. There is no visible programme to turn that into a repeatable seeding motion, and Bengaluru has 5+ creators above 100K.
11. **No hero-product strategy.** Konbi proves one cross-section-photogenic item beats a broad menu. Fillo has 100+ SKUs and no single visual icon. The eggless shokupan pull-apart or the anpan cream cross-section are the obvious candidates.
12. **Best story is untold.** "300+ customers tested pre-launch" and the difficulty of an eggless shokupan (the fuwa fuwa texture normally depends on egg) is a genuine R&D narrative — exactly the material Krish Ashok–style food-science creators amplify.
13. **The truck is a content asset being treated as logistics.** A moving bakery generates native BTS content daily — route reveals, 4am bake, sell-outs, neighbourhood reactions. Competitors with fixed premises cannot make this content. Nobody in Bengaluru is contesting this niche.
14. **Brand name undefended** on Facebook, X, Threads, TikTok.
