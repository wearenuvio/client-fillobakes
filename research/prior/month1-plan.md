# Fillo Bakes × Nuvio — Month One, Week by Week
_The first four weeks of the engagement, built from the touchpoint matrix and the primary-phase execution research. Owner tags: **N** = Nuvio, **F** = Fillo (founders/ops), **J** = joint._

---

## Week 0 — Kickoff (one 45-minute call, before week 1 starts)

Decisions that gate everything downstream. Founders commit to a **48-hour approval SLA** here — the research is blunt that founder decision latency, not design time, is what stretches a 2-week identity into 5.

1. **Pick the world** — Maneki Pop / Shōwa Press / Catch the Van (or the mix: character + game mechanic + quiet pack). The moodboards, samples and kits exist for exactly this call. **[F decides]**
2. Confirm platforms, posting cadence tolerance, and who on Fillo's side shoots raw footage on bake days. **[J]**
3. Hand over accounts: IG admin, Google account for GBP, Shopify, domain email plan. **[F]**
4. Confirm FSSAI licence status + GST/PAN docs location (gates listings AND labels). **[F]**
5. Approve the Week-1 quick-win list below. **[J]**

## The five threads, at a glance

| Thread | Wk 1 | Wk 2 | Wk 3 | Wk 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | Platform + brief | Concept gate + kraft test | Refinement gate | Final files + guidelines |
| **Instagram** | Profile foundation | Quiet re-seed (4 posts) | Ramp (5 posts) + collabs | Announce + steady state (7/wk) |
| **WhatsApp** | Number + Channel | Catalog + opt-in | Growth loop + broadcasts | List segments working |
| **Google/reviews** | Create + verify + FSSAI | Fill profile + plumbing | Listings + first reviews | 15–25 review pace check |
| **Packaging** | Track-A artwork | Track-A ships (branded week 2) | — | Track-B briefed + gift spec |

**Running underneath, every week:** handwritten note in every box, two bell rings at every stop, Diwali corporate outreach (week 1 onward — the window is closing), founder 48h approval SLA.

**Budget frame for the month (excl. agency fee):** lean ₹73k / recommended ₹1.53L — the delta is mostly identity depth and packaging quality.

**What month one deliberately does NOT do:** van wrap production (needs locked Pantone + BBMP permission — files week 4, wraps in month 2), WhatsApp API spend, the subscription, the site reskin. Sequence over speed.
