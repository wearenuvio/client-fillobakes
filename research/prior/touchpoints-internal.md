## 1. The journey — where a Bengaluru consumer actually meets Fillo

Model: fresh-bakery D2C with route model. Seven stages. Every touchpoint below is real — observed in audit or structurally required by the model.

```
DISCOVER → CONSIDER → ORDER → WAIT → RECEIVE → EAT/SHARE → REPEAT/ADVOCATE
```

**Two journey types:**
- **A. Digital-first** (dominant today): IG/press/search → website → checkout → doorstep delivery
- **B. Street-first** (the truck's own funnel): sees van on road / at society gate → QR/handle → follows or buys on the spot → converts to digital repeat

Journey B is almost entirely unbuilt. It's also the one no competitor can copy.

## 2. Touchpoint inventory — deep dive

Format per touchpoint: **current state → what it must reflect → requirement → data anchor → owner/effort**

### STAGE 1 — DISCOVER

#### T1. The truck itself (rolling OOH)
- **Current:** unbranded/undesigned as media; zero presence in content; a van doing logistics.
- **Must reflect:** the entire brand in 3 seconds at 30 km/h — Japanese-pop × Bengaluru warmth, "moving bakery" legibility, follow/order instruction.
- **Requirement:** full livery system — wordmark + mark, signature color block visible 50m away, "Bengaluru's Moving Japanese Bakery" claim line, giant QR + @fillo_bakes + WhatsApp number, fuwa-fuwa visual motif. Design once, photographs forever: livery IS the content set-dressing for every reel.
- **Data anchor:** 64% of consumers noticed a wrapped vehicle in the past month — the most-noticed *moving* OOH format (Nielsen/OAAA 2019, High conf). Bengaluru branded-van media rents at ₹12–25k/van/day (Vigyapan Mart 2026) — Fillo's van is on the road daily anyway, so the wrap buys that media value at ≈₹0 marginal cost. (Do NOT quote the "97% recall" figure — untraceable 3M folklore per benchmarks doc.)
- **Owner/effort:** design Nuvio, wrap production client capex (~₹40–80k one-time typical for van wrap India). Effort M.

#### T2. Instagram @fillo_bakes
- **Current:** 5,132 followers, 0 original posts in 6 mo, 1 original reel ever, following 0.
- **Must reflect:** alive, craveable, truck-led spectacle; fuwa-fuwa texture proof; weekly rhythm people plan around.
- **Requirement:** relaunch cadence 3–4/wk (60–70% reels), 3 series: "Where's the truck?" geo-drop, jiggle/ASMR crumb, eggless blind-taste; outbound engagement 20–50 local accounts; reactivate Sharmaji Eats + Sudha Rao.
- **Data anchor:** ~60% discover restaurants via IG (Electro IQ 2025); 74% say social guides dining choices (Restroworks 2025); **sends carry ~3–5x the weight of likes for non-follower reach** (Mosseri/Instagram 2025) → optimize for send-worthy content (jiggle clips are send-native); 79% say UGC highly impacts buying vs 13% brand content (Stackla); milk-bread trend live (Waitrose +617%); rival posts 56x more with 1/4 the audience.
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio core. Effort H (ongoing).

#### T3. Google surface (GBP + local pack + organic)
- **Current:** no GBP found, zero reviews, no title tags site-wide, invisible for "shokupan Bangalore".
- **Must reflect:** existence, hours, route model explained, rating ≥4.5, drool photos.
- **Requirement:** GBP created + verified (category: Bakery + Delivery), photo set, Q&A seeded ("do you deliver to X?"), review loop feeding it; site titles/meta/schema fixed; /shokupan into nav+sitemap; neighbourhood pages.
- **Data anchor:** 97% read reviews for local businesses; **65% have rejected a business over reviews — including having none** (BrightLocal 2026); **+1 Yelp star → 5–9% revenue, effect exists only for independents** (Luca/HBS — the single best causal stat in the research, and it applies exactly to Fillo); complete GBP with photos → +42% direction requests, +35% site clicks (Google platform data).
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio (SEO track) + client owns GBP account. Effort M, mostly one-time + loop.

#### T4. Press & creators (earned)
- **Current:** The Hindu + Market Spoon + News Karnataka + 2 voluntary creator reels. All unleveraged — not on site, not pinned, no follow-up programme.
- **Must reflect:** third-party validation at every decision moment.
- **Requirement:** press bar on homepage, "As seen in The Hindu" on truck + packaging sticker, pinned highlight, quarterly creator seeding motion (5 Bengaluru creators >100K identified in socials.md).
- **Data anchor:** 79% say UGC highly impacts purchase vs 13% for brand content and 8% for influencer content (Stackla) — repost rights > paid posts; 2 warm relationships already exist = free restart.
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio. Effort L.

#### T5. Marketplace shelves (Swiggy listing "Fillo Bakes Plus")
- **Current:** live, Ramamurthy Nagar, rating unverified, likely default images/copy.
- **Must reflect:** brand consistency + funnel to own channel (packaging insert does the migration).
- **Requirement:** audit listing, hero images matching brand system, correct naming (why "Plus"?). Treat as discovery-only channel — 25–35% take makes ₹129–159 items near-breakeven.
- **Owner/effort:** client account, Nuvio creative. Effort L.

### STAGE 2 — CONSIDER

#### T6. Website first impression (home + shop)
- **Current:** functional but generic v0 aesthetic; wordmark only; "100+ items" claim vs 23 SKUs; strong checkout under weak shell.
- **Must reflect:** premium Japanese craft + trustworthiness; the truck story above the fold; press proof.
- **Requirement:** brand-system reskin (design tokens from new identity), truck + founders visible, press bar, real review embeds, consistent claims, product photography standard (crumb shot).
- **Owner/effort:** design Nuvio; implementation client dev (site dev out of Nuvio scope — flag). Effort M.

#### T7. Product pages / copy
- **Current:** duplicate copy (3 sandos identical), no sensory language system, no trust line, no schema.
- **Must reflect:** taste in words; eggless/FSSAI/fresh-after-order trust stack; Japanese story per category (anpan 1874 / Emperor Meiji myth).
- **Requirement:** unique sensory description per SKU + trust line + Product schema + story block per category.
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio (SEO/content track). Effort M one-time.

#### T8. Reviews & social proof layer
- **Current:** 8 unverifiable testimonials; on-site review widget goes nowhere; ~zero public reviews anywhere.
- **Must reflect:** real people, real names, real platforms.
- **Requirement:** post-delivery review ask (48h, WhatsApp link to GBP), embed live Google reviews on site, UGC repost rights loop.
- **Data anchor:** on-site reviews → **up to +270% conversion, steepest lift from 0→5 reviews, optimum 4.0–4.7 stars** (Spiegel/Northwestern 2017) — Fillo sits at exactly 0, i.e. the highest-leverage point on the curve; the first 5 reviews per surface are the whole game.
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio lifecycle + client ops. Effort L, compounding.

### STAGE 3 — ORDER

#### T9. Checkout flow
- **Current:** GOOD — slots, FILLO10, Fillo+ upsell, Shopify. Missing pincode/area check; policies contradict model (courier boilerplate).
- **Must reflect:** certainty — "we reach you, here's when, here's the van."
- **Requirement:** pincode/area checker pre-cart; rewrite shipping policy around van + slots; delivery-day expectations copy.
- **Owner/effort:** client dev; Nuvio copy. Effort L–M.

#### T10. WhatsApp (ordering rail + broadcast)
- **Current:** contact-page button only; press says WhatsApp is core pre-order channel; NO Channel, no broadcast list, no catalog.
- **Must reflect:** the storefront-in-pocket; "truck in Indiranagar 4pm, 12 loaves left."
- **Requirement:** WhatsApp Business catalog + Channel launch; opt-in at checkout, on truck QR, on packaging; weekly drop broadcast + route alerts.
- **Data anchor:** WhatsApp cart recovery converts **18–25% vs 3.66% for email** (Interakt platform data, India — best-provenanced WhatsApp stat found); Channels hit 500M+ MAU in year one with **India as #1 market and zero algorithmic reach suppression** (Meta 2024–25); plan broadcasts against a realistic **3–7% conversion** (ignore 45–60% vendor claims); micro-bakery customers already trained to pre-order via WhatsApp/IG DM (market.md).
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio lifecycle + client number ownership. Effort L to launch. **Highest leverage-to-effort ratio in the whole map.**

### STAGE 4 — WAIT (order → doorstep)

#### T11. Order confirmation & van-tracking page
- **Current:** live GPS tracker exists (15-sec updates!) but buried as corner pill; no order-day link ("your bread is 2 stops away").
- **Must reflect:** theatre of the moving bakery — anticipation as entertainment.
- **Requirement:** promote to /track flagship page: route calendar, live pin, "notify me when near me" opt-in; link it in order confirmation + WhatsApp.
- **Data anchor:** Mister Softee productized exactly this across 625 trucks; "where is the truck" is a normalized consumer expectation (usp-analogues.md).
- **Owner/effort:** client dev + Nuvio spec/design. Effort M. **Most differentiating single feature Fillo owns.**

### STAGE 5 — RECEIVE

#### T12. Packaging (REQUIREMENT — per client)
- **Current:** unknown/undesigned (no packaging visible in any brand surface; no packaging system exists).
- **Must reflect:** the entire brand at the only moment 100% of customers touch it. Japanese gift-grade cues (Ginza Nishikawa: "packaging is designed for gifting"); eggless/FSSAI trust marks; story (anpan 1874 card); re-order rail (QR → WhatsApp).
- **Requirement:** full system — branded sleeve/box per category, "don't toast, tear it" ritual card, sticker set, press sticker, QR insert (review ask + Fillo+ + WhatsApp opt-in), gift 2-pack format ₹600–900. Design for the unboxing shot: packaging is the #1 UGC generator for food D2C.
- **Data anchor:** 72% say packaging design influences purchase; 63% say paper/card signals premium (Ipsos 2018, n=2,002); **61% more likely to rebuy after premium packaging, 60% won't rebuy after poor packaging, 40% share unboxings** (55% Gen Z/millennial) (Dotcom Distribution); India: **₹1.8 lakh crore Diwali gifting spend, 53% prefer sweets/bakery/chocolate as gifts** (LocalCircles 2024); Ginza Nishikawa sells $18 loaves substantially on pack + gifting.
- **Owner/effort:** design Nuvio, production client capex. Effort M–H one-time. **The margin lever: gift-grade pack moves price ceiling.**

#### T13. Doorstep handoff moment
- **Current:** undesigned — delivery happens, no script, no uniform, no moment.
- **Must reflect:** the neighbourhood-bread-vendor nostalgia the founders themselves cite; human face of the brand.
- **Requirement:** minimal kit — branded apron/tee, handoff line, warm-loaf cue, "van bell" audio signature (the old bread-vendor bell = nostalgia trigger + audible brand asset, costs nothing, deeply on-story), **handwritten thank-you note in every first order**.
- **Data anchor:** handwritten note → **+100.5% subsequent customer spend** ($25.97→$52.07, n=1,232 RCT, J. Interactive Marketing 2022) — photocopies work as well as originals (scalable), machine-printed doesn't, and **the effect dies if paired with a discount** (so: note, no coupon). 85% won't reorder after a poor delivery experience; 65% will pay more after a good one (Ipsos/Bringg 2025).
- **Owner/effort:** client ops, Nuvio playbook. Effort L. **Cheapest high-confidence lever in the entire map — promote to earlier execution even though ops-owned.**

### STAGE 6 — EAT / SHARE

#### T14. The product as content (crumb, jiggle, cross-section)
- **Current:** zero owned texture content despite "fuwa fuwa" in bio and global jiggle-ASMR trend mid-cycle.
- **Must reflect:** proof of texture claim = the whole purchase promise.
- **Requirement:** hero-shot standard (Konbi model: one photogenic icon — shokupan pull-apart or anpan cross-section), shot spec for every SKU, eat-ritual card drives "do the pull-apart" UGC.
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio creative. Effort M ongoing.

### STAGE 7 — REPEAT / ADVOCATE

#### T15. Fillo+ loyalty
- **Current:** mechanics live (₹1 join, ~2% back) but zero nurture, weak vs Blue Tokai 10%; no named perk.
- **Must reflect:** insider status + frequency engine (Panera: price so member must come weekly to "win").
- **Requirement:** welcome flow, points-balance pings via WhatsApp, permanent absurd perk ("free anpan on every drop"), member early access to drops.
- **Data anchor:** loyalty members: **+40% spend per visit, +64% visit frequency** vs non-members (450+ restaurant study via Square 2024); redeemers spend 3.1x non-redeemers — nudging redemption matters as much as earning. Email flows drive ~41% of email revenue from 5.3% of sends (Klaviyo); F&B abandoned-cart flows convert 3.66% (Klaviyo 143k+ flows).
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio lifecycle. Effort M.

#### T16. Weekly drop ritual
- **Current:** "Weekly Specials" tab EMPTY. No drop time, no cap, no waitlist.
- **Must reflect:** scarcity as honesty ("what we can bake"), one hour one day forever.
- **Requirement:** named weekly moment (e.g. "The Thursday Bake, 6pm"), hard cap, countdown, sold-out post, waitlist→next-week forecast. Margin programme disguised as marketing (waste 8%→4%).
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio + client production planning. Effort M.

#### T17. Subscription (standing order) — future core engine
- **Current:** doesn't exist. Market.md: subscription cohorts 2.5–3x LTV, 2x 180-day retention — corroborated and slightly conservative: food subscriptions run **3–4x LTV (8–18 orders vs 1–1.5)** (Eightx 2026/McKinsey). Sour House proves Bengaluru demand; society-gate density model (Milkbasket 20–30 orders/gate).
- **Requirement:** weekly shokupan standing order tied to fixed truck stop; society unlock mechanic (X orders opens your gate).
- **Owner/effort:** joint, quarter-horizon. Effort H.

#### T18. Referral / gifting
- **Current:** none. Eggless = "safe to gift" reframe unlocks festival/office occasions (market.md).
- **Requirement:** gift pack SKU + festival calendar (Diwali eggless gifting) + simple give-a-loaf referral.
- **Data anchor:** ₹1.8 lakh crore Diwali gifting spend, **53% prefer sweets/bakery/chocolate as gifts** (LocalCircles 2024); referral programmes: 3–5% median conversion, referred customers +16–25% LTV, +37% retention (ReferralCandy/Extole 2026). **Diwali 2026 = Nov 8 — gift SKU must exist by mid-October; this pulls T18 forward from pure tertiary.**
- **Owner/effort:** joint. Effort M, seasonal timing.

### CROSS-STAGE

#### T19. Brand identity system (feeds T1, T6, T12, T14 — everything)
- **Current:** none. Text wordmark, emoji as design.
- **Requirement:** logo + mark, color/type system, sticker kit, motif ("fuwa fuwa" cloud device?), photo style, tone of voice reconciling cozy-nostalgia × Japanese-pop precision. This is the dependency root: truck wrap, packaging, feed templates, site reskin all block on it.
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio design system track. Effort H one-time. **Sequence first.**

#### T20. YouTube / LinkedIn / secondary platforms
- **Current:** YT empty; LinkedIn wrong page type, 5 followers.
- **Requirement:** repurpose reels → Shorts (search-answering titles: "eggless shokupan", "Japanese milk bread India"); convert showcase→company page; founder posts for B2B/HORECA lane.
- **Owner/effort:** Nuvio. Effort L (repurposing engine).

## 3. Priority matrix

_Lens: blend of revenue speed × brand leverage. Bucket = focus order, not calendar — some tertiary items are cheap and can ride along._

### PRIMARY — "Exist credibly and convert" (weeks 1–4)
| # | Touchpoint | Why primary | Solves |
|---|---|---|---|
| T19 | Brand identity system | Dependency root for truck, pack, feed, site | Nothing compounds without it |
| T2 | Instagram relaunch (truck-led) | 5.1K idle followers + live global trend + 60% discovery share | Dead-brand perception; wasted audience; discovery |
| T10 | WhatsApp Channel + catalog | Highest leverage:effort; owns the ordering rail; free | Retention rail; drop distribution; margin (vs 25–35% marketplace take) |
| T3 | GBP + reviews loop + SEO basics (titles/meta/schema/sitemap) | Zero-review + zero-title state is actively blocking conversion today | Trust at decision moment; local discovery; the "is this real?" problem |
| T12 | Packaging system | 100% customer reach; client-named requirement; UGC generator; price-ceiling mover | Brand memory; repurchase trigger; gifting revenue; premium pricing |

**Bucket solves:** the credibility gap. Today a curious customer checks IG (dead), Google (absent), reviews (none) and receives an unbranded product. Primary bucket makes every first encounter confirm the press instead of contradicting it — and installs the owned channels (IG + WhatsApp + GBP) that everything later runs on.

### SECONDARY — "Weaponize the differentiators" (weeks 4–10)
| # | Touchpoint | Why secondary | Solves |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Truck livery | Needs T19 first; capex; then free daily OOH forever | Street-level awareness; journey B funnel; content set-dressing |
| T11 | /track flagship + notify-me | Needs dev; converts the tracker into an acquisition loop | Differentiation nobody can copy; opt-in list growth |
| T16 | Weekly drop ritual | Needs audience warm (T2) + rail (T10) first | Demand certainty; waste→margin; habitual re-visit |
| T8 | Review/UGC engine + press bar on site | Runs on T3's GBP + T12's QR insert | Compounding social proof; SEO local signals |
| T7 | Product copy + schema + sensory system | Conversion polish on pages that now get traffic | Ranking caps; page conversion; story depth |
| T4 | Creator seeding motion | Warm relationships exist; amplifies T2 content | Reach beyond 5K; borrowed trust |
| T9 | Pincode check + policy rewrite | Small dev fix; removes silent order-blocker | Checkout abandonment; expectation mismatch |

**Bucket solves:** differentiation and demand rhythm. Primary makes Fillo credible; secondary makes it *unmistakable* (truck, tracker, drop ritual) and converts attention into a weekly habit with compounding proof.

### TERTIARY — "Compound and scale" (quarter horizon)
| # | Touchpoint | Why tertiary | Solves |
|---|---|---|---|
| T17 | Weekly subscription + society-gate unlock | Needs habit + density data from drops first | LTV engine (2.5–3x); route economics; wastage → zero |
| T15 | Fillo+ nurture + named perk redesign | Rides on WhatsApp rail once list exists | Frequency; member status; repeat rate |
| T13 | Doorstep handoff kit + van bell | Ops-dependent — **but handwritten note (+100.5% spend, RCT) pull forward: start week 1, costs nothing** | Memorability; nostalgia story made physical; word-of-mouth |
| T18 | Gifting SKU + festival calendar + referral | Seasonal windows — **Diwali Nov 8 2026 → gift SKU ready mid-Oct, plan in secondary window** | New occasions; AOV; eggless-as-gift positioning (53% of Indians prefer sweets/bakery as gifts) |
| T6 | Full site reskin | Needs T19 tokens; client dev capacity | Brand coherence; premium perception |
| T14 | Hero-shot content standard | Formalizes what T2 starts organically | Iconic product recognition (Konbi effect) |
| T5 | Swiggy listing polish | Low-margin channel; discovery-only role | Channel consistency; funnel to own rail |
| T20 | YouTube Shorts + LinkedIn fix | Pure repurposing; B2B lane optional | Search video queries unanswered; HORECA pipeline |

**Bucket solves:** economics and scale. Turns the habit built in secondary into contracted revenue (subscription), higher AOV (gifting), lower CAC (referral/UGC flywheel), and channel breadth — the moat phase.

## 5. What we tell the client each bucket buys (plain language)
- **Primary:** "When someone hears about you, everything they check confirms you're real, premium, and alive — and we now own three direct lines to them (IG, WhatsApp, Google)."
- **Secondary:** "The things only you have — the truck, the live tracker, the weekly bake — become the reasons people talk about you and come back on schedule."
- **Tertiary:** "Habit becomes contract: subscriptions, gifting, referrals. Marketing cost per order falls while order value rises."
