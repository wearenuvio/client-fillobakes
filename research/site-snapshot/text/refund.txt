Refund & Return Policy 
At Fillo Bakes , we take pride in delivering fresh, high-quality baked snacks. However, if something doesn’t go right, we’re here to help. 
Eligibility for Refunds 
Due to the perishable nature of our products, refunds are only offered in the following situations: 
Item delivered in damaged or unusable condition 
Incorrect product delivered 
Severe quality issues (must include photo/video proof) 
How to Request a Refund 
To request a refund, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with: 
Your order ID 
Clear photos of the product and package 
A short description of the issue 
Refund Approval 
Once approved, refunds will be issued back to your original payment method via Razorpay. Processing typically takes 7–10 business days , depending on your bank. 
Cancellations 
Orders can be cancelled before dispatch . To request cancellation, contact us immediately at: wiseeatsindia@gmail.com
