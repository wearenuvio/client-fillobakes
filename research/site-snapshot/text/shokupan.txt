Shokupan – Japanese Milk Bread 
The iconic Japanese bread known for its cloud-like softness, subtle sweetness, and perfect slice. 
 Shokupan bread is a classic Japanese milk bread celebrated for its incredibly soft crumb, delicate flavor, and refined baking technique. At Fillo Bakes, we craft our Shokupan using slow fermentation, premium dairy, and precise temperature control to achieve its signature texture. 
What Makes Shokupan Special? 
Ultra-soft, fluffy interior with fine crumb structure 
Lightly sweet and rich from milk and butter 
Perfect for toast, sandwiches, or eating plain 
No preservatives, baked fresh in small batches
