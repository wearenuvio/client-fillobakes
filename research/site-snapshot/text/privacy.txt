Privacy Policy 
Fillo Bakes ("we", "us" or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information. 
Information We Collect 
Contact information (name, email, phone, shipping address) 
Order history and purchase details 
Payment information (processed via Razorpay — we do not store full card data) 
Analytics and device information 
How We Use Your Information 
We use your information to process orders, provide customer support, improve the website, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. 
Sharing & Third Parties 
We share data with service providers who perform services on our behalf (payment gateways like Razorpay, shipping partners, analytics providers). We will never sell your personal data. 
Security 
We implement reasonable security measures. Payments are handled by Razorpay using their secure systems. We do not store full payment card details on our servers. 
Your Rights 
You can request access, correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at wiseeatsindia@gmail.com .
