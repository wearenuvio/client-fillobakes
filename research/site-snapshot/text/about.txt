About Fillo Bakes 
Bringing nostalgia and precision baking to your doorstep 
Our Story 
 Fillo Bakes was founded by Neha S Nirmal and Nischal Vasant Meethal in December 2025, with a mission to revive the nostalgia of neighbourhood bread vendors. We wanted to bring back that warm memory of fresh bread reaching your doorstep—the way milk vendors do today. Drawing from years of experience in the F&B space, we launched a 100% vegetarian, Japanese-inspired moving bakery that celebrates fresh, handcrafted breads made with precision and care. 
Our Vision 
Growing up, many of us remember the warmth of local bakeries: the smell of fresh bread in the evening, picking up pav for dinner, or buns for tea. We believe that fresh bread can become a daily staple, just like milk subscriptions. Our moving bakery concept brings that nostalgia back—delivering fresh, handmade breads directly to your neighbourhood, with every batch baked fresh after your order arrives. 
What Makes Us Different 
✓ 100% Vegetarian Japanese Baking — precision-crafted, never compromised 
✓ Signature "fuwa fuwa" (pillowy) texture — achieved through precision hydration, fermentation timing, and technique 
✓ Made completely eggless without sacrificing softness or texture 
✓ Small-batch, hand-rolled, and baked fresh after your order 
✓ Intentionally tight menu with signature offerings: shokupan, karepans, and anpans 
✓ Flavors developed from travels to Japan and inspired by real kitchens, not chemicals 
Our Specialties 
Our menu celebrates Japanese-style baking with Indian sensibilities. Our shokupan is a signature soft milk bread that took extensive refinement to perfect in an eggless format. Our karepans feature crispy outsides and cloud-soft insides with innovative fillings like umami-forward vegetables and slow-cooked ratatouille. Our anpans are soft buns filled with fresh cream in flavours ranging from chocolate to banana biscoff. We also offer toasts, pies, strudels, and seasonal innovations—all made with the same commitment to quality and precision. 
Our Mission 
Our mission is to create fresh, handcrafted breads that are delicious, wholesome, and made for every moment. We're obsessed with precision baking, texture consistency, and delivering that warm, familiar feeling of neighbourhood bakeries. Whether you're starting your day, taking a tea break, or hosting a special moment, Fillo Bakes is here to make every bite unforgettable. 
Thank You for Believing in Us 
Your support means everything to us. From the rigorous testing phase with over 300 first-time tasters to every order today, we remain committed to the craft of precision baking. We're always exploring new flavours, seasonal innovations, and expanding to more neighbourhoods. Thank you for being part of this journey—the best is truly yet to come!
