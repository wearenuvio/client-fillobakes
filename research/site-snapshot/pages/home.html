Japanese Breads & Global Pastries at Fillo Bakes

At Fillo Bakes, we specialize in artisanal vegetarian pastries inspired by global baking traditions. From soft Japanese breads to flaky European strudels, every product is handcrafted using premium ingredients.

Our bakery is especially known for Japanese Shokupan bread, a milk bread celebrated for its cloud-soft texture and subtle sweetness. Shokupan is a staple in Japanese households and is perfect for toast, sandwiches, and gourmet creations.

Alongside Japanese breads, we also offer a curated range of savory and dessert strudels, blending traditional techniques with modern vegetarian flavors.

Explore our full menu →

What is Shokupan Bread?

Shokupan is a traditional Japanese milk breadknown for its ultra-soft crumb and rich, buttery flavor.

At Fillo Bakes, our Shokupan-inspired breads are made fresh and pair beautifully with sweet or savory toppings.

Our Signature Strudels

Strudels are flaky pastries layered with delicate fillings. Our vegetarian strudels range from spiced apple desserts to bold savory creations.

Each strudel is baked to achieve the perfect balance of crisp layers and rich fillings.

Our Story ✨

At Fillo Bakes, we believe every pastry tells a story. We started with a simple dream: to bring joy to people through handcrafted, delicious pastries that celebrate global flavors.

From Japanese An Pan to Korean Kare Pan, Indian spices to Mediterranean flavors — every bite is a journey around the world. We use only the finest vegetarian ingredients, bake fresh daily, and pour our hearts into every creation.
